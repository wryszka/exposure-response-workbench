# Story Cards

**Nine short stories, each one person, one moment, one number. Each stands on its own — pick the ones that fit the room.**

How to use them:
- **5 minutes:** cards 1 → 2 → 6. *(Alert, watch it spread, the buyer at the window.)*
- **10 minutes:** cards 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 6.
- **"Wow" add-on:** card 5, if the room is engaged.
- **For a technical or data room:** add card 7 (the batch we refused) and card 9 (the agent team).
- **For underwriters:** add card 8 (one rule, two workbenches) after card 6.
- Every number below is what the screen shows — read it off the screen, don't recite it.
- Plain words only. No platform names unless someone asks.

---

## 1 · "The fire nobody's told you about yet"

**Who:** the exposure manager, first thing in the morning.
**Today:** someone notices a fire on the news, exports the policy list, finds a fire map, and overlays them by hand. Hours.
**Show:** the app opens on the alert board. Point at the 🔥 **Var wildfire** card. Nothing else.
**Say:** *"Before anyone's asked, there's an alert: a fire, and these are our homes in its path."*
**The number:** **59 homes · €164m at risk.**
**Why it lands:** from "find out hours later, by hand" to "it tells you".

---

## 2 · "Watch it spread"

**Who:** the same exposure manager, now the fire is growing.
**Today:** a static map, refreshed by re-doing the overlay.
**Show:** **Track ▸** → **Track live on the dashboard**. Let it advance two or three steps (or press **Next update ▸**).
**Say:** *"Every dot is a home we insure. Red means it's in the fire's path — and it's updating as the data arrives."*
**The number:** **0 → 73 → 90 → 105 homes**, **€287m** at full size. Point at the live-ingestion counter ticking.
**Why it lands:** it's not a picture of yesterday — it's moving while you watch.

---

## 3 · "One flood, two countries"

**Who:** the European exposure team, split by country.
**Today:** Italy sees its claims, Austria sees its claims — nobody sees that it's one event. *(This is exactly the miss from the recent Italy / France / Austria floods.)*
**Show:** back to the alert board → 🌊 **Alpine flood** → **Track ▸** → the by-country split.
**Say:** *"One flood. Italy and Austria together, in one alert — not two teams finding out separately."*
**The number:** **122 homes — 69 in Italy, 53 in Austria. €331m. One alert.**
**Why it lands:** the cross-border blind spot disappears.

---

## 4 · "What does it actually cost us?"

**Who:** the head of exposure or the CFO.
**Today:** someone builds a spreadsheet after the event.
**Show:** in an event's view, the cost card (cover at risk → what reinsurance pays → what we keep).
**Say:** *"Our reinsurance takes the big hit. What we keep on this fire is €25m."*
**The number:** Var fire — **we keep €25m.**
**If they push:** a small fire stays under what reinsurance covers (we keep it all, €13.7m); a severe windstorm blows through the top of the programme (we keep €232m). The calculation follows the treaty, it isn't fixed.
**Why it lands:** the money question is answered while the event's still happening.

---

## 5 · "We knew before the satellite did"

**Who:** the exposure manager, before any official feed has caught it.
**Today:** you wait for the satellite or the official warning.
**Show:** **Event Radar** — the press report with the ⚡ "beats the feed" badge.
**Say:** *"A news report mentioned a fire. We checked it against our book — it touches our homes — fourteen hours before the satellite confirmed it."*
**The number:** **36 homes · €104m · 14 hours early.** Promoting it to an alert is a person's click, not the system's.
**Why it lands:** not just faster — earlier. And a human still decides.

---

## 6 · "The buyer at the window"

**Who:** someone online, buying home insurance — with the fire visible from their window.
**Today:** the quote system has no idea there's a fire. The policy gets sold. The claim comes in next week.
**Show:** **Get cover** → 🔥 **Villa in the Var** → quote → **Buy cover now** → **Declined**. Then 🏡 **Apartment in Munich** → quote → **Buy** → **Approved**.
**Say:** *"Same product, same rule. The house in the fire zone — no. The flat in Munich — yes. Decided by what's happening right now."*
**The number:** Var — **declined, in the active zone.** Munich — **approved, €33,573 a year.**
**Why it lands:** everyone understands this one instantly. It's the punchline — finish on it.

---

## 7 · "The batch we refused"

**Who:** the data or exposure team, the morning after a feed went wrong.
**Today:** bad data flows straight into the spreadsheet; someone notices days later, if at all.
**Show:** **Explore → Data sources & quality** — the red *rejected* batch at the top.
**Say:** *"Overnight a weather feed sent us coordinates the wrong way round. The system refused the batch, kept yesterday's good data, and told us why."*
**The number:** **38% of records outside Europe — limit 5%. Rejected, quarantined.**
**Why it lands:** live data you can actually trust — the system says no to bad data, not just to bad risks.

---

## 8 · "One rule, two workbenches"

**Who:** an underwriter quoting a commercial property in the UK.
**Today:** the underwriting system has no idea there's a live flood warning on the street.
**Show:** the **underwriting workbench** → submission **Calder Valley Fine Foods** (Hebden Bridge) → the *Disaster zone (live)* check.
**Say:** *"The flood team publishes the live warning zones. Underwriting checks every quote against them. This mill is inside an Environment Agency warning — refer, don't quote."*
**The number:** **inside the zone — refer, quote blocked.** A Cheshire risk 45 km away quotes normally.
**Why it lands:** the same live picture protects the whole business, not just one team.

---

## 9 · "Ask the team"

**Who:** the head of exposure, wanting an answer without opening five screens.
**Today:** an email to three people and a wait.
**Show:** **Explore → AI agents** → ask *"What's my exposure to the Var wildfire, gross and net?"*
**Say:** *"Each agent has one job. The supervisor sent this to the reinsurance specialist — and every number comes from the governed calculation, not from the AI."*
**The number:** **59 homes · we keep €25m** — answered by the Reinsurance Analyst.
**Why it lands:** fast answers, with a clear line on what the AI may and may not do.
